CONTOSO RETAIL – STORE & EMPLOYEE FEEDBACK
Workshop Sample – Fictional Data

1. New York – Store Manager
I spend most Monday mornings combining sales, staffing, inventory, and incident information into a weekly update. A tool that prepares a first draft and highlights exceptions would save significant time, but I need to know where every number came from.

2. London – Department Manager
Employees frequently ask where to find the latest returns, warranty, and promotion guidance. Search works, but people do not always know the correct document name.

3. Singapore – Customer Service Lead
New team members take longer to answer product and policy questions. An assistant could help, provided it only uses approved and current information and makes escalation easy.

4. Dubai – Inventory Manager
We usually notice some shelf availability problems after a store team reports them. Earlier identification of unusual inventory movements could help us investigate before customers are affected.

5. Johannesburg – Store Manager
We already receive many alerts. Another system generating alerts without prioritization would create more noise. Any AI solution should explain why an issue is important.

6. Sao Paulo – Regional Operations Lead
Reporting formats differ by store. Regional teams spend time normalizing updates before leadership reviews them. Consistent AI-assisted summaries could help.

7. Toronto – Customer Service Manager
Speed matters, but a fast wrong answer is worse than a slower correct answer. Customer-facing recommendations need clear sources and human review for sensitive cases.

8. Sydney – Store Manager
I would support an AI pilot if it reduces administrative work without creating another system that staff need to maintain manually.

COMMON THEMES
- Reduce repetitive reporting and information searching.
- Improve inventory exception detection.
- Provide trusted, source-based answers.
- Avoid excessive alerts and low-confidence recommendations.
- Maintain human oversight.
- Demonstrate measurable time savings before scaling.
